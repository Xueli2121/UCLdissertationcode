# Method and software attribution

The Adaptive+ calibration equations implemented in `src/structure_only_adaptive_plus.py` follow the method developed by Matteo Sesia, Y. X. Rachel Wang and Xin Tong.

The implementation was developed with reference to the authors' official MIT-licensed repository:

https://github.com/msesia/conformal-label-noise

The following components are specific to the present dissertation:

- the controlled structure-only misspecification path;
- the paired Oracle-versus-misspecified simulation design;
- the worst-class clean coverage analysis;
- the comparison of discrepancies in T, M and V;
- the signed algorithm-aware diagnostic G_Aplus;
- the scenario-level bootstrap and grouped validation route;
- the final thesis figures and reporting structure.

The original MIT licence is retained in `LICENSE`.
